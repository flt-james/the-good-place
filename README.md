# The Good Place

Three arguments about AI, power, and what happens next.

## The arguments

1. **[The Earth Today](THE_EARTH_TODAY.md)** — what's happening right now. Model commoditisation, the orchestration layer, and the race to own the interface you think through.

2. **[The Bad Place](THE_BAD_PLACE.md)** — where the current trajectory leads. Manipulation surfaces, enshittification of critical services, and the conversion of economic power into political power.

3. **[The Good Place](THE_GOOD_PLACE.md)** — what any credible alternative needs to get right. Three criteria, backed by historical evidence, that any response has to satisfy.

Read them in order. They're not long.

## The research

The arguments are backed by detailed research files in [`research/`](research/). These go deep on the evidence.

## How to engage

Read the documents. For anything you disagree with, dig into the research and chase it down until either you're convinced or you've found something to contest.

AI helps here. Clone the repo, fire up your AI tool of choice, and point it at an argument file. Get it to summarise, then interrogate the claims against the evidence.

The repo includes [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) — an adversarial review prompt designed to help you stress-test the arguments. If your tool uses a project instructions file (e.g. `CLAUDE.md`, `.cursorrules`), rename it and point your agent at the repo.

If you want to contest something — please do — but at least read the relevant section of the actual document first, so you're not arguing with a hallucination.
