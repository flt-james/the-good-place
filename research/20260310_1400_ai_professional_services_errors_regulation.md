# AI in Professional Services: Current State, Errors, and Regulation

**Research Date:** 10 March 2026

---

## 1. AI in Legal Services

### Harvey AI
- AI platform for law firms: document analysis, legal research, workflow automation.
- 1,000+ customers in 60+ countries, including CMS (7,000+ lawyers) and HSBC.
- Funding trajectory: $100M Series C (July 2024, $1.5B valuation) → $300M Series D (Feb 2025, $3B) → $300M Series E (June 2025, $5B) → $160M Series F (Dec 2025, ~$8B).
- Investors include OpenAI, Sequoia Capital, Kleiner Perkins.

### Documented Legal AI Errors
- **486 documented cases** of legal AI hallucinations worldwide (324 in US courts).
- **156 instances** of lawyers sanctioned for AI hallucinations in court filings.
- Stanford research: legal AI tools hallucinate in **1 out of 6+ queries** (>17% error rate for Lexis+ AI and Westlaw AI).
- MyPillow/Lindell case: attorneys fined $3,000 each for AI-generated fake citations.
- Australian solicitor prohibited from unsupervised practice for 2 years.
- Canadian lawyer faced contempt of court sanctions (Ko v Li, 2025).

### Cost Impact
- Contract review: **10 hours → 2 hours** with AI.
- Tacit Legal's "Tilder": contracts from ~**GBP 95** (AI analysis + senior lawyer sign-off).
- BUT: **6 in 10** in-house professionals saw **no savings in time or money** from AI use by outside lawyers.
- **40%+** said corporate clients have **not yet demanded pricing adjustments**.
- **Key finding: Cost reductions are largely captured as law firm margin, not passed to clients.**

Sources:
- [Stanford HAI](https://hai.stanford.edu/news/ai-trial-legal-models-hallucinate-1-out-6-or-more-benchmarking-queries)
- [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/in-house-counsel/ai-does-little-to-reduce-law-firm-billable-hours-survey-shows)
- [AI Hallucination Cases Database](https://www.damiencharlotin.com/hallucinations/)

## 2. AI in Healthcare

### Abridge
- Transforms patient-clinician conversations into AI-generated clinical notes (Epic EHR integration).
- 100+ health systems: Duke Health, Johns Hopkins, Mayo Clinic, UNC Health.
- $300M Series E, $5.3B valuation. ~30% of ambient scribe market.

### Economics
- McKinsey: AI could save **$200-360 billion annually** in US healthcare.
- AI was **4x more likely** than doctors to arrive at correct diagnosis in challenging cases.
- NHS England: **GBP 250 million** saved 2022-2024 from AI chatbots/automation.
- **60%+** of hospital networks using AI report reduced operating costs (up to **12% annually**).
- Implementation costs: **$500K-$5M** per enterprise solution.

### Documented Errors
- **14% increase** in malpractice claims involving AI tools in 2024 vs 2022.
- April 2025 Nature Medicine: AI diagnostic tools changed recommendations based on patient race, gender, income — not symptoms.
- Some pediatric studies: AI misdiagnosis rate exceeded **80%**.
- DOJ subpoenaed digital health companies regarding generative AI in EMR systems (2024).

Sources:
- [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01722-y)
- [UCSF CoDEX](https://codex.ucsf.edu/news/editors-pick-study-finds-ai-medical-tools-show-bias-potential-misdiagnosis-and-patient-harm)
- [Abridge](https://www.abridge.com/)

## 4. AI in Education

- Human tutoring: **$25-100/hour**.

- Harvard study: AI tutoring in physics achieved **double learning outcomes at 40% less cost** ($49 vs $85/session).

## 5. AI in Software Development

- GitHub Copilot: tasks completed **55.8% faster**; up to **81% productivity improvement**.
- Pull request time: **9.6 days → 2.4 days** (75% reduction).
- 2026: **84% of developers** use AI tools; AI writes **41% of all code**.
- Copilot: **20 million users** by July 2025; **90% of Fortune 100**.
- Caveat: **46% of developers** don't fully trust AI outputs; only ~30% of AI-generated code accepted.

Sources:
- [Index.dev](https://www.index.dev/blog/developer-productivity-statistics-with-ai-tools)
- [GetPanto](https://www.getpanto.ai/blog/github-copilot-statistics)

## 6. Regulatory Responses

- **EU AI Act**: Entered force August 1, 2024. Most healthcare AI classified "high-risk." Full application August 2, 2026.
- **Bar Council of England and Wales**: January 2024 warning on AI reliance risks "incompetence or gross negligence."
- **Law Society (UK)**: May 2025 "Generative AI: The Essentials" — AI literacy as baseline professional competence.
- **Texas AG**: Settlement against company selling AI healthcare documentation for "false, misleading" accuracy claims.

- [EU AI Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai)
