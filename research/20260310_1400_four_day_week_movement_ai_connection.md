# Four-Day Week Movement & AI Connection

**Research Date:** 10 March 2026

---

## 1. Scale of the Movement

Since 2019, **4 Day Week Global** has coordinated trials in **10+ countries** across six continents. Standard model: **100:80:100** (100% pay, 80% time, 100% output). **92% of participating companies** kept the four-day week after trials ended.

---

## 2. Trial Results by Country

### UK (June-December 2022)
- **61 companies**, ~2,900 workers.
- **39%** less stressed; **71%** reduced burnout.
- Staff turnover dropped **57%**.
- As of October 2025, **11% of UK workforce** (2.7 million) report four-day schedule.
- 12-month follow-up published in *Nature Human Behaviour* (July 2025): wellbeing remained high (not novelty effect).

### Iceland (2015-2019)
- Two trials: **2,500 workers** (1%+ of working population).
- 35-36 hour weeks, no pay reduction.
- Productivity constant or **increased**.
- Today: **86% of Iceland's working population** have shorter weeks or right to negotiate them.
- Iceland's economy expanded **5% in 2023** (second only to Malta among rich European economies).

### Spain
- Valencia trial: **35% reduction** in stress; **64%** increased sleep.
- **EUR 50 million** EU-funded national pilot through 2027 (200 firms, 32-hour weeks).
- **May 2025**: Government approved bill reducing standard week from **40 to 37.5 hours** without pay cuts — **12 million+ private sector workers** affected.

### Germany (2024-2025)
- Participants slept **38 minutes more/week**, exercised **25 minutes extra**, reduced stress by **~90 minutes**.

### US & Canada
- 35 companies, ~2,000 employees: revenue rose **8% during trial**, **37.55% increase** vs previous year.
- Burnout decreased **0.44 points** on 1-5 scale (71% reduction in symptoms).

## 3. The AI-to-Four-Day-Week Connection

- **29%** of organisations with four-day workweeks use AI extensively, vs **only 8%** of five-day organisations.
- **Convictional** (12-person software startup): moved to 32-hour week because AI absorbed "a large amount" of manual work.
- **Game Lounge** (marketing firm): AI tools → claimed **22% increase in output**.
- Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang: AI will "probably" bring four-day weeks.
- Zoom CEO Eric Yuan: AI automation could make five-day weeks unnecessary.
- Fortune (January 2026): "Could 2026 be the year of the 4-day workweek?"
- Washington Post (December 2025): "AI is helping these companies pull off a four-day workweek"

## 4. The Kellogg's Warning

The four-day week movement faces the same structural risk as Kellogg's six-hour day: without ownership of the structure, the benefit can be clawed back.

- Kellogg's workers voted overwhelmingly for six hours. It was still reversed over 50 years.
- The forces that killed it: management ideology, union trade-offs, rising fixed employment costs, cultural shift equating long hours with virtue.
- **The structural difference**: if workers own the firm (cooperative), there is no management to impose an ultimatum. The time dividend is a decision by worker-owners, not a concession from employers.

The four-day week as employer-granted perk = Kellogg's (reversible).
The four-day week as worker-owner choice = structural (durable).
