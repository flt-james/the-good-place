## 1. Frances Haugen / Facebook Files (October 2021)

Key internal research findings:
- **13.5%** of UK teen girls said suicidal thoughts became more frequent after starting Instagram.
- **17%** of teen girls reported eating disorders worsened.
- **32%** of teen girls who felt bad about their bodies said Instagram made them feel worse.
- **2018 algorithm change**: Facebook deliberately shifted to amplify content provoking strong emotions (especially anger) because it drove engagement. Internal research confirmed increased polarization.
- **2019 internal report**: European political parties felt the algorithm forced them to "skew negative," pushing toward more extreme policy positions.

## 2. Algorithmic Feed Manipulation Research (2023-2026)

### Feed Reranking Experiment (Science, 2024)
- Reranking feeds on X to decrease/increase partisan animosity shifted out-party feelings by **2+ points on 100-point scale** in just **10 days** with 1,256 participants during 2024 US election.

### X/Twitter Algorithmic vs. Chronological (Science, 2023)
- Switching from chronological to algorithmic feed shifted users toward **more conservative political positions** on policy priorities and views on Trump investigations and Ukraine.

### Facebook/Instagram
- Switching from algorithmic to chronological feed **reduced time spent** on platforms.

### TikTok
- Hyper-personalisation reduced content diversity by **32%**, with **42%** of heavy users experiencing "genre stagnation."

Sources:

- [Nature (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10098-2)

## 3. AI Chatbot Influence on Decision-Making

### Cornell University Study (2025)
- AI chatbots programmed to advocate for specific candidates moved opposition voters' preferences by **10+ percentage points**.
- Pro-Harris chatbot moved likely Trump voters **3.9 points** toward Harris — roughly **4x the effect** of traditional political ads tested during 2016 and 2020 elections.
- **Mechanism**: LLMs persuade by generating high volume of supporting claims (not necessarily accurate). Arguments built on accurate claims can still mislead by omission.

### Emotional Dependency
- High usage of chatbots for advice/decision-making is associated with high emotional dependence and "problematic use."

## 4. Key Structural Insight

Algorithmic feeds manipulate **attention** — what you look at.

AI chat interfaces manipulate **decisions** — what you do.

The manipulation surface is qualitatively larger because the interface participates in reasoning, not just information presentation. A captured chat interface can surface some options and not others, frame choices to favour the platform, route tool calls to preferred third parties, and shape information order and tone — all invisibly.

The Cornell study demonstrates this is not theoretical: AI chatbots are already **4x more effective at shifting political preferences** than traditional advertising.
